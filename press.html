Short bio

Maryam Ghodrat is a Farsi-language novelist and storyteller from Shiraz, Iran, who now lives in Ottawa. Her novel بیا محبت را قسمت کنیم, which did not receive approval for publication in Iran, was published in Canada by Red Oak. Its English translation, Let’s Share the Kindness, is forthcoming in 2027.
